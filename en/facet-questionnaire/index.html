of facet origin

Orientation questionnaire based on the items in the DLF document — Diagnosis of Low Back Pain of Facet Origin.

Source: Dr Jean-Edouard Loret, Medical thesis, University of Tours, 2013.

Important: this questionnaire cannot establish a diagnosis on its own. The source document does not provide an interpretation threshold for the score. The “Clinical examination” section should be performed and interpreted with a healthcare professional.

No diagnostic threshold in the DLF questionnaire is considered conclusive on its own for facet syndrome. The higher the score, the greater the likelihood of having facet syndrome. The result should therefore be discussed with a physician and considered alongside the clinical examination and the full diagnostic approach.
